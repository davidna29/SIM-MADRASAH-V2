# Surface brief — Website Publik Madrasah

## Scope and mode
Public website on the primary domain: home, profile, organization, teachers, facilities, news, agenda, gallery, video, contact, privacy, PPDB, and transfer-registration flows. Mode: Persuade + Read.

## Audience and outcome
Families and community members need a trustworthy, quick overview of the madrasah, current information, and the available admission paths. Keep identity, news, agenda, and admissions balanced; never invent claims or display unreviewed demo content as real.

## Chosen direction
Extend the established Mading world as a living public noticeboard: keep the madrasah green, warm paper, Plus Jakarta Sans, and familiar UI components; move away from an admin-like wall of identical cards toward editorial hierarchy, generous image fields when real approved photos exist, and a typographic board composition until assets are supplied. Keep factual proof and metrics hidden until verified.

## Interaction and content
Five grouped navigation destinations; keyboard-accessible desktop menus and mobile drawer; one clear primary action per viewport. News and upcoming agenda lead the reading path; photography/video appear only when genuine public media exists. PPDB and transfer forms show progress, step-local validation, a clear privacy notice, and a stored guardian consent record.

## Constraints and open decisions
No approved logo or real photos are currently in the database; use no stock or synthetic school imagery. Per-person staff publication is opt-in. Do not expose student identifiers or sensitive staff fields. Privacy copy and any accreditation/program/statistics content must be reviewed by madrasah staff before production launch.
